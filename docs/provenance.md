# Provenance and scope

The stage structure abstracts an existing research workflow: question → literature → measurement → design → diagnostics → robustness → evidence-linked writing. The public implementation is newly written and does not copy the private workflow engine, manuscript, data or model results.

The author identified the two English research tracks. Method and revision records were inspected to abstract destination-choice and coauthorship-recurrence workflows; this is a process mapping, not a new replication of either paper. Spatial design guidance, QGIS automation, public templates and synthetic examples are additions in this package. Their availability does not establish validation on real research.

QGIS is an external dependency with its own license. The official documentation links are references, not bundled software. No external basemap or administrative boundary is redistributed.

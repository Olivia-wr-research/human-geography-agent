# Human Geography Research

First identify the research question, current stage, authorized data and outputs. When continuing a project, inspect its manifest and authoritative artifacts; do not restart from a stale draft. This skill supports a research assistant, not autonomous publication certification.

Read [workflow.md](references/workflow.md) for stage contracts. Read [methods.md](references/methods.md) when choosing spatial units, measurements or models. Read [qgis.md](references/qgis.md) for maps and [writing.md](references/writing.md) for manuscripts and public packages.

## Operating rules

- Define unit, spatial extent, period, mechanism and claim type before choosing a model. Do not force all human geography questions into quantitative or causal designs.
- Preserve raw data; assign stable IDs and retain exclusions, conflicts and unresolved cases. Keep names/addresses out of public examples.
- A roster association, publication affiliation and verified move are different measurements. Geographic aggregation does not establish individual behavior.
- A zero needs observation support; missing or unmatched records are not zeros by default. Restrict predictors to information available before the event.
- Freeze primary specifications and robustness rationale before inspecting preferred significance. Changes need rationale and versioned outputs.
- Check model convergence, support and uncertainty before interpreting coefficients. Failed fits remain failed, never silently replaced by convenient results.
- Link every substantive claim to an artifact and location. Label unresolved evidence. Search and verify cited sources; do not invent references.
- Separate execution and review as explicit passes. Review underlying artifacts, not only an executor summary. Do not claim independent review unless another reviewer actually performed it.
- At each formal stage, present evidence and unresolved decisions to the researcher. Record only their actual approval, revision or rejection. Existing authorization governs routine execution within a stage.
- A passed script test does not certify research validity, journal fit or publication readiness.

## Starting request

Ask only for missing information that changes the work: question, dataset access, spatial/time unit, intended output. Complete independent tasks while waiting. Use the smallest sufficient model and preserve qualitative or mixed-method routes where appropriate.

## Handoff

Report current stage; inputs and hashes; code/parameters; produced artifacts; review findings; pending author decisions; and the next reproducible command. No automatic submission, account actions or external messaging are implied.
